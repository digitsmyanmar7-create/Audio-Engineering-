Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our audio engineering services

What file formats do you accept for mixing/mastering?

We accept WAV, AIFF, MP3, and FLAC files. For mixing projects, we prefer 24-bit WAV files at the original sample rate of your project. For mastering, we need the final stereo mix as a 24-bit WAV file with -6dB of headroom.

How long does a typical mixing or mastering project take?

Mixing: 3-5 business days per song. Mastering: 2-3 business days per song. Rush services are available for an additional fee. Complex projects or albums may require additional time, which we'll discuss before starting.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! We include up to 2 rounds of revisions for mixing projects and 1 round for mastering. Additional revisions are available for a small fee. We want you to be completely satisfied with the final product.

What's your pricing structure?

Our pricing is project-based. Mixing starts at $150 per song, mastering at $75 per song. We offer package deals for albums and EPs. For custom projects (sound design, film audio, etc.), we provide quotes after discussing your specific needs.

Can I attend the mixing/mastering session remotely?

Absolutely! We offer remote session attendance via Zoom or Source-Connect. This allows you to listen in real-time and provide feedback as we work. We schedule remote sessions in advance to ensure we have dedicated time for your project.
